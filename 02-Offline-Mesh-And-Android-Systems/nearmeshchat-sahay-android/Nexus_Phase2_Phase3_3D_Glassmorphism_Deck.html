02. Executive Problem Statement & Mesh Vision
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Off-Grid Crisis
  • Cellular Grid Collapse: Disasters & power blackouts destroy phone towers completely.
  • Cloud App Shutdown: WhatsApp & Telegram freeze without active cloud servers.
  • Proximity Isolation: Mobile phones 10m apart cannot talk without cell towers.
The Nexus Solution
  • 100% Serverless & Zero SIM: Pure P2P radio communication (Wi-Fi Direct & BLE 5.0).
  • Dynamic Multi-Hop Relay: Mobile phones automatically relay encrypted packets.
  • Full Off-Grid Suite: Text Chat, Voice, 720p Video Calls & RuVNet 3D Radar.

05. RuVNet Wi-Fi Wave Sensing 3D Radar
AI SENSING
Wave Sensing Technology
  • Camera-Free Detection: Detects human presence through walls & darkness without cameras.
  • Signal Disturbance Analysis: Measures multipath Wi-Fi signal reflections caused by body movement.
  • Disaster Search & Rescue: Locates trapped victims in rubble while preserving 100% privacy.
3D Radar Pipeline
  • 1. Emission: 2.4GHz / 5GHz Wi-Fi radio wave propagation.
  • 2. Perturbation: Motion induces phase shifts & wave reflections.
  • 3. Extraction: Algorithmic extraction of Doppler variance.
  • 4. Render: X, Y, Z coordinates plotted on tactical radar.
06. UI Design & Emerald Green (#16A34A) Rationale
UI SYSTEM
Emerald Green Rationale
  • High Sunlight Contrast: Emerald green wavelengths offer optimal outdoor daylight legibility.
  • Night Rescue Visibility: Low eye strain during night rescue operations vs harsh white light.
  • OLED Power Efficiency: Deep slate (#0F172A) backdrop saves up to 35% AMOLED battery.
App UI Architecture
  • Mesh Navigation Bar: Quick toggles between Mesh Chat, Calls, RuVNet Radar & SOS Suite.
  • Status Header: Real-time peer counter & Wi-Fi Direct state.
  • Call Control Overlay: Floating video feed preview with live latency readout.
