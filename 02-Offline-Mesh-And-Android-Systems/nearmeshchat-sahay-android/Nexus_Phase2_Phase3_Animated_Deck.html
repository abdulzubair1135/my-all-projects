🚨 02. Executive Problem Statement & Vision
MOTIVATION
🚨 The Off-Grid Crisis
  • Cellular Network Outages: Disasters, power blackouts & remote zones leave populations isolated.
  • Server Dependency: Apps like WhatsApp or Telegram freeze completely without cloud servers.
  • Proximity Isolation: Standard smartphones 10 meters apart cannot communicate without cell towers.
🌐 The Nexus Mesh Solution
  • 100% Serverless & Zero SIM: Pure P2P radio hardware communication (Wi-Fi Direct & BLE 5.0).
  • Self-Healing Mesh Topology: Dynamic multi-hop relay routes around collapsed nodes automatically.
  • Complete Off-Grid Suite: Encrypted Chat, Voice Calls, 720p Video, RuVNet Radar & SOS Beacon.

📡 Dynamic Peer Discovery & NSD
  • Simultaneous Wi-Fi Direct P2P scan & Bluetooth LE 5.0 beaconing.
  • Network Service Discovery (mDNS) auto-resolves local mesh IP endpoints.
  • Zero manual pairing needed — devices handshake automatically in range.
📦 Packet Structure & Store-Forward
  • Unique Packet Hash ID prevents infinite network loops and flooding.
  • Store-and-Forward Queue buffers messages until recipient comes into range.
  • Managed TTL (Time-To-Live) ensures zero network congestion.
