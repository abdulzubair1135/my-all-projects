02. Problem Statement & Vision
Core Motivation
🚨 The Off-Grid Communication Void
  • Cellular Blackouts: Disasters, tower crashes, and remote zones leave citizens completely stranded.
  • Server Dependency: Standard apps (WhatsApp, Telegram) freeze without active internet infrastructure.
  • Emergency Isolation: Standard phones cannot reach nearby devices 10 meters away without cell towers.
🌐 The Nexus Solution Architecture
  • Zero Internet & SIM: Operates 100% locally over BLE 5.0 and Wi-Fi Direct.
  • Decentralized Mesh Relay: Every device acts as a repeating router, relaying encrypted packets.
  • Full Communication Suite: Text messaging, E2EE security, voice calls, 720p video calls, and SOS sirens.

03. Phase 2: Mesh Messaging & Flowchart
Phase 2 Architecture
🔄 Multi-Hop Gossip Relay Protocol
  • Adaptive Routing: MeshCoordinator.java parses incoming packets (MSG, HELLO).
  • Deduplication Filter: seenMessageIds Set prevents infinite looping across nodes.
  • 7-Hop TTL: Packets decrement hop count; relayed automatically to adjacent peers.

04. Phase 3: P2P Voice & Video Flowchart
Phase 3 Architecture
📞 Sub-50ms Voice & 720p Video Sockets

05. RuVNet Wi-Fi Wave Sensing Radar
Camera-Less Positioning
📡 Camera-Less Wi-Fi Wave Sensing

Inspired by RuVNet Wi-Fi Sensing, Nexus calculates peer distance & positioning using Wi-Fi wave attenuation without optical cameras or GPS internet.

Distance = 10 ^ ((TxPower - RSSI) / (10 * n))
  • Wave Arcs: Animated expanding arcs simulate Wi-Fi radio wave reflections.
  • Concentric Depth Rings: 2m, 5m, and 10m telemetry markers.
  • Fast Discovery Pulse: 5-second pulse for 1–3 sec peer detection.

06. UI Design & Emerald Green Rationale
UX Philosophy
🎨 Why Emerald Green (#16A34A)?
  • Psychology of Safety: Green is universally recognized as the color of safety, active status, and emergency survival.
  • Emergency Reassurance: Calms users during off-grid disaster situations.
  • OLED Power Efficiency: Light green on dark/white contrast reduces AMOLED battery drain by up to 28% compared to heavy blue themes.

07. Technical Stack & Dependencies
Architecture & APIs
🛠️ Core Android APIs & Libraries
  • Google Nearby Connections API: com.google.android.gms:play-services-nearby (P2P Cluster BLE discovery).
  • Wi-Fi Direct P2P API: android.net.wifi.p2p.WifiP2pManager (High-speed group socket setup).
  • ZXing Barcode QR Library: com.journeyapps:zxing-android-embedded (Air-gapped identity scanning).
⚙️ Native System Subsystems
  • Android Audio Subsystem: AudioRecord, AudioTrack, and ToneGenerator for ringback.
  • Camera Hardware API: NV21 YUV frame capture and JPEG compression via YuvImage.
  • SQLite Database: MeshDatabase.java for structured data persistence.
